Combat

  • When fighting, keep your cross hair on your opponent. Preferably at head length.
  • If you have a big health advantage, it is okay to start a 50/50.
  • Aim duel MUCH more.
  • You need to micro adjust. A micro adjust is when after you flick, you don't just shoot. You "micro adjust"/move a tiny bit to make sure your shot is on point.
  • If you hold out your blueprint onto someone elses build and it turns to a lock instead of an arrow, that means they are editing it.
  • The best way to start a fight with someone that is in the open is to run up to them until you are about 35 meters away and then shoot them.


Building and Editing

Building:

  • Side Jump with Cover: Place two stairs going up and then put a cone at the top of the second stair. Then, on the second stair, jump to the side and make two more stairs. Repeat.
  • Before engaging with someone, block off all signs of danger.
  • If a person is under you spraying you or has no cover, you can jump and shoot them; preferably with a pump, and then immediately place a floor. Because of delay, they won’t know what hit them.
  • If someone is in a box with no cone or stair and if there is no floor or cone in front of their box, you can look a bit under their wall and phase a cone in their box. Then, predict their reaction on where they will go and box them up.
  • Don’t box yourself in wood.

Editing:

(I am going to be combining editing with other stuff to leave out confuseness)

(Editing)

  • When you do an edit, finish it at your opponent so you have a momentum when shooting them.
  • If you are in a box and edit a wall, edit your cone toward that wall so no one can break through easily.
  • If someone is at an elevation in a box and you have a wall, you vcan do a four tile edit and make it so you can see their legs but they can barely see you. (You have to be a bit away from the wall).
  • The bottom left three tile edit is NOT good.
  • If your own build is hiding from a different build, you can blueprint edit to edit it again.
  • You can bait someone by doing a top row edit and then crouching.
  • You can do a side-wall edit to rotate.
  • Use window edits to place cones in opponents boxes.
  • It is very good to use a right window edit and move to the left or a left window edit and move to the right.

(Piece Control)

  • Plan out your piece control before you just run in and go with your gut.
  • The person that is doing the movement has the advantage
  • When you see someone with only 1 wall in their box, get their back, side, and then front wall. You can also struggle to get a cone in there. Then just piece them up with a diagonal peak or a window edit.

(Peaking)

  • If you are standing on a cone and your opponent is in the middle of their box or your box, you can do a wall edit where you open the bottom right 2 spots and then also one up from the bottom right. Now if you are still standing on the cone you can shoot them but they won’t see you.

(Phasing)

  • On your left side, make a door facing towards you and then go to the side of the open door. Then hit your opponents box.


Other

Movement:

  • On flat ground you are running 5.5 m/s, but going up elevation you are actually running 7.8 m/s.
  • Anytime you land with momentum, you have to jump instantly upon sliding (slide jumping) a couple times.
  • Slide jumping at a decline is about 50% faster than normally siding. (Be careful not to take fall damage).
  • On flat ground, just sprinting is faster than sprint jumping.
  • The fastest way to rotate on flat ground is to sprint, slide (2x), and jump (2x)
  • Grappling downwards at an elevation and slide jumping is faster than grappling far at an elevation.

Sound:

  • Footsteps can be heard from 35 meters away max.
  • Sliding can be heard from 60 meters away max.
  • Taking out your pickaxe can be heard from 30 meters away max.
  • Pulling out a gun can be heard from 20 meters away max.
  • Most weapons can be heard from 275 meters max.
  • Most heals have a visual sound noice of 5 meters and can be heard from 20 meters away max.
  • Cars have a visual sound noise of 70 meters and can be heard from 120 meters max. Cars have the same visual sound noice whether you are boosting, driving, or being idle.
  • Building and editing can be heard from 40 meters away max.
  • Breaking builds can be heard from 60 meters away max.
  • You can be heard falling from 20 meters away max.
  • Turning your character while standing makes a sound.
  • Uncrouching makes a sound.

Pings:

  • If you ping a car and someone is inside it, it will show the ping icon instead of the car icon.
  • The "help" ping (Squad Requests) lasts forever; you can "help" ping ammo near someone you just killed and if their teammate or someone random picks up the ammo, the ping will turn off. You can also "help" ping ammo in a vault or old tarp so you know when someone is there.


Ranked

Overall:

  • Match Quests, Bosses, Satellite Towers, and Loot Island give you rank percentage.
  • Get 3rd party kills because players fighting will be low on health.
  • If there is more than one other person, fight outside your POI or location. What I mean by this is don’t go in the middle of your location so you don’t get third parties very hard. Stay on the outskirts and 3rd party but from a distance.
  • Stop relying on placement points and get some kills.
  • Dealing damage gives you %. Even if someone steals your kill, if you deal most of the damage, you will probably gain more percent from that player than them.

Mid Game:

Late Game:

  • Don't be greedy; if you have what you need, don't try to go for more.
